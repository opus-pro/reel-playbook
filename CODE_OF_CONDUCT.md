# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, whatever their background or level of experience. Be respectful, assume good intent and give feedback people can act on.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, sustained disruption and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. That includes feedback on someone's videos: critique the work, not the person.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, or block someone from taking part, when it breaks these expectations.

To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's **Security** tab and say it's a conduct report. Maintainers will review every report promptly and protect the reporter's privacy.
